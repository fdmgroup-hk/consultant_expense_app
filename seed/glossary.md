# Capital Markets Glossary for New Consultants

Terms you will hear in your first week. Expand an acronym the first time you use it in an interview — it shows you understand it rather than having memorised the letters.

## Market participants and structure

**Buy side** — asset managers, hedge funds, pension funds. They buy financial services and invest capital.
**Sell side** — investment banks and brokers. They provide liquidity, execution, research and structuring.
**Broker-dealer** — an entity that both executes for clients (broker) and trades its own capital (dealer).
**Market maker** — quotes a two-way price and is obliged to trade at it, providing liquidity.
**Prime broker** — provides financing, custody, stock lending and consolidated reporting to hedge funds.
**Custodian** — holds securities on behalf of a client and services them (settlement, corporate actions, income).
**CSD / ICSD** — Central Securities Depository; the definitive register of ownership. Euroclear, Clearstream, DTC, CCASS.
**CCP** — Central Counterparty; clearing house that novates trades and takes margin. LCH, Eurex Clearing, HKSCC.

## Instruments

**Equity** — ownership in a company.
**Bond / fixed income** — a loan to an issuer paying coupons and returning principal at maturity.
**Coupon** — the interest payment on a bond. **Yield** — the return implied by the current price.
**Duration** — sensitivity of a bond's price to interest-rate moves.
**FX spot / forward / swap** — currency exchange now, at a future date, or a combination of both legs.
**Future** — an exchange-traded, standardised contract to buy or sell at a set price on a set date.
**Forward** — the same idea, but bilateral and customisable (OTC).
**Option** — the right, not the obligation, to buy (**call**) or sell (**put**) at a **strike** price.
**Swap** — an exchange of cash flows. An interest rate swap exchanges fixed for floating.
**ETF** — a fund that trades like a single stock.
**Repo** — sell a security now and agree to buy it back later; economically a secured loan.
**Securities lending** — lending stock for a fee, often to enable short selling.

## Trading and execution

**Bid / offer (ask)** — the price someone will buy at / sell at. **Spread** — the difference.
**Order book** — the resting bids and offers at each price level.
**Fill / partial fill** — an execution against your order, whole or in part.
**Slippage** — the difference between the expected price and the achieved price.
**Market impact** — the price movement your own order causes.
**Liquidity** — how easily you can trade size without moving the price.
**VWAP / TWAP** — Volume/Time Weighted Average Price; both a benchmark and a family of algorithms.
**Dark pool** — a venue with no pre-trade transparency.
**SOR** — Smart Order Router.
**OMS / EMS** — Order / Execution Management System.
**FIX** — Financial Information eXchange, the messaging protocol for orders and executions.
**Blotter** — the screen showing a desk's orders and trades.

## Post-trade

**Trade capture** — booking the execution into the bank's records.
**Enrichment** — attaching reference data to a raw trade.
**Confirmation / affirmation** — agreeing the terms with the counterparty.
**Novation** — replacing one counterparty with another, typically the CCP.
**Netting** — collapsing many obligations into one net figure.
**Margin** — collateral posted against risk. **Initial margin** up front, **variation margin** daily against mark-to-market.
**Margin call** — a demand for more collateral.
**DvP** — Delivery versus Payment; securities and cash move together.
**T+1 / T+2** — settlement one or two business days after trade date.
**Fail** — a trade that does not settle on the intended date.
**Buy-in** — forcing the purchase of securities in the market to close out a persistent fail.
**Nostro / vostro** — "our account with you" / "your account with us".
**Reconciliation (rec)** — comparing two sets of records. A **break** is a difference that needs investigating.
**Corporate action** — dividend, split, merger, rights issue. **Mandatory** or **voluntary** (requiring an election).
**SSI** — Standard Settlement Instructions: where to deliver cash and securities.

## Risk and control

**Mark to market** — revaluing a position at the current market price.
**P&L** — profit and loss. **P&L explain / attribution** — decomposing the daily move into its causes.
**VaR** — Value at Risk; the loss not expected to be exceeded at a given confidence over a period.
**The Greeks** — sensitivities of an option's value: delta (underlying price), gamma (delta's own change), vega (volatility), theta (time), rho (rates).
**Counterparty risk** — the risk they do not pay.
**Market risk** — the risk prices move against you. **Credit risk** — the risk of default. **Operational risk** — the risk of failed processes, people or systems. (Technology failures are operational risk, which is why controls are strict.)
**Limit** — a cap on exposure. A **breach** must be reported.
**Four-eyes principle** — a second person must approve.
**Segregation of duties** — the person who builds is not the person who releases to production.
**Chinese wall / information barrier** — enforced separation between parts of the bank with conflicting information.

## Identifiers and reference data

**ISIN** — 12-character global security identifier.
**CUSIP** — US/Canadian identifier. **SEDOL** — UK identifier. **RIC** — Refinitiv code. **Ticker** — the exchange symbol.
**LEI** — Legal Entity Identifier; identifies the legal party to a trade.
**MIC** — Market Identifier Code; identifies a venue.
**Book** — a portfolio owned by a desk, against which trades are recorded.
**Golden source** — the authoritative system for a piece of data.

## Regulation

**MiFID II / MiFIR** — EU/UK rules on transparency, best execution, transaction reporting and clock synchronisation.
**EMIR** — EU derivatives reporting, clearing and risk-mitigation rules.
**Dodd-Frank** — the US post-crisis reform act.
**CSDR** — EU settlement discipline, including cash penalties for fails.
**Basel III / IV** — bank capital and liquidity standards.
**KYC / AML** — Know Your Customer / Anti-Money Laundering.
**SFC / HKMA** — Hong Kong's Securities and Futures Commission and Monetary Authority.
**FCA / PRA** — the UK's conduct and prudential regulators.
**Best execution** — the obligation to take reasonable steps to obtain the best result for the client.

## Technology and delivery

**SDLC** — Software Development Lifecycle.
**Change record (CR/RFC)** — the approved authorisation to alter production.
**CAB** — Change Advisory Board.
**Release window / freeze** — when changes may and may not be deployed. Year-end freezes are common.
**Back-out plan** — how you undo a change that goes wrong. Required, not optional.
**SIT / UAT** — System Integration Testing / User Acceptance Testing.
**RTB / CTB** — Run the Bank (keep it working) / Change the Bank (build new things).
**BAU** — Business As Usual.
**SLA** — Service Level Agreement.
**RCA** — Root Cause Analysis.
**Sev 1 / P1** — the highest incident severity.
**Golden copy** — the master version of a dataset.
**Batch window** — the overnight period in which scheduled processing must complete.
